Spring is the perfect time to give your home a fresh start. In North Carolina, it's also pollen season, when a yellow layer seems to cover everything. These tips will help you clean thoroughly without bringing harsh chemicals into your home.

## Start From the Top

Work from ceiling to floor. Dust ceiling fans, light fixtures, and the tops of cabinets first, then shelves and furniture, and finish with the floors. That way, nothing you knock loose lands on a surface you've already cleaned.

## Keep Pollen Outside

Pollen sneaks in on shoes, clothes, pets, and open windows. Wipe down windowsills and door tracks, wash entry mats, and vacuum often during peak season. A damp microfiber cloth picks up pollen better than a dry duster, which tends to push it back into the air.

## Refresh Soft Surfaces

Curtains, throw pillows, bedding, and upholstery hold onto dust and allergens all winter. Wash what you can, and vacuum the rest with an upholstery attachment.

## Swap in Simple Natural Cleaners

You don't need strong chemicals to get a home clean:

- **White vinegar and water** for glass and many hard surfaces (avoid natural stone)
- **Baking soda** as a gentle scrub for sinks, tubs, and stovetops
- **Castile soap** for floors and general-purpose cleaning
- **Microfiber cloths** to trap dust instead of spreading it

## Don't Forget the Overlooked Spots

Baseboards, light switches, door handles, vents, and the space under furniture are easy to skip. Spring is the time to get them all.
