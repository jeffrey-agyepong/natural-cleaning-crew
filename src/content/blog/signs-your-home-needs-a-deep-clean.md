Day-to-day tidying keeps things looking neat, but dust and grime build up in places that quick cleanups miss. Here are five signs it's time for a deep clean.

## 1. Dust Comes Back Almost Immediately

If you dust a shelf and it looks dull again within a day or two, dust has likely settled in vents, ceiling fans, baseboards, and soft furnishings. A deep clean tackles those sources instead of just the surfaces you can see.

## 2. Your Home Has a Lingering Smell

Odors that hang around after you've cleaned often come from places that rarely get attention, like behind appliances, inside trash cans, under rugs, or in upholstery. Covering them with air fresheners only adds more fragrance to the mix.

## 3. Allergy Symptoms Act Up Indoors

Sneezing or itchy eyes at home can be a sign that dust, pet dander, and pollen have built up in carpets, cushions, and bedding. Thorough vacuuming and dusting with non-toxic products can make a real difference in how your home feels.

## 4. Bathrooms and Kitchens Never Look Quite Clean

Soap scum, hard water spots, and greasy film on cabinets and backsplashes don't come off with a quick wipe. These surfaces need focused scrubbing, which is exactly what a deep clean is for.

## 5. A Big Change Is Coming

Moving in or out, hosting family for the holidays, bringing home a new baby, or starting a new season are all great times to reset your home from top to bottom.

A deep clean every few months makes regular upkeep easier. Using natural products means you get that fresh-start feeling without the harsh chemical smell.
